Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026. FinForge is made by Kologboh LLC.

The short version

FinForge helps you track your money and learn how it works. To do that, it keeps the things you put into it, and nothing more.

What we keep

  • Your first name, email, and birth year
  • The budgets, spending, and goals you enter
  • Which lessons you've finished
  • Whether you have Premium

What we never collect

  • Bank or card details
  • Your location
  • Contacts or photos
  • Data for ads or tracking

We don't sell your information, we don't show ads, and you can delete your account and everything in it from the app at any time.

Who we are

FinForge is a money app for teenagers, built and run by Kologboh LLC, a company based in Jacksonville, Florida. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Kologboh LLC, and "you" means anyone who uses FinForge.

Who FinForge is for

FinForge is designed for people aged 13 and older in the United States. When you sign up, we ask for your birth year, and we don't let anyone younger than 13 create an account.

If we learn that someone under 13 has created an account anyway, we will delete the account and its information. If you're a parent and believe your child under 13 has signed up, please contact us.

What we collect

Your account

Your first name, email address, password, and birth year. Your password is stored by our authentication provider in a scrambled (hashed) form that we can't read. We use your birth year only to confirm you're old enough to use FinForge.

The money information you enter

FinForge doesn't connect to any bank. Everything in it is typed in by you: how much money you have each month, the budget categories you create and the amounts you assign them, the spending you log (amount, category, and any note you add), and your savings goals. Notes are free text, so we'd suggest not writing anything in them you wouldn't want stored.

Your learning progress

Which Learn modules you've completed and how many quiz questions you got right.

A parent or guardian's email, if you add one

You can choose to add a parent or guardian's email address in Settings. We store it with your account. We do not currently send any emails to that address. If we add features that do, we'll update this policy first.

Subscription details

If you subscribe to Premium, Apple handles the payment. We receive and store the details needed to know whether your subscription is active: which plan you chose, a transaction ID from Apple, the subscription's status, and when the current period ends. We never see or store your payment card.

On your device

FinForge stores a sign-in token on your phone so you stay logged in. It doesn't use advertising IDs, analytics tools, or tracking of any kind.

How we use it

  • To run the app: showing your budget, spending, goals, and lessons.
  • To confirm your Premium subscription with Apple and unlock Premium features.
  • To send you account emails you ask for, like a password reset code.
  • To keep FinForge secure and fix problems.

We don't use your information for advertising, we don't build profiles of you for anyone else, and we don't sell it.

Who we share it with

We only share information with the companies that help us run FinForge, and only what they need to do that job:

  • Supabase stores your account and app data and handles sign-in and password reset emails.
  • Railway hosts our server, which confirms subscriptions with Apple and processes account deletions.
  • Apple processes Premium payments through the App Store, under Apple's own privacy policy.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety. If Kologboh LLC is ever sold or merged, this policy would continue to apply to your information.

Subscriptions and payments

FinForge Premium is sold through Apple's App Store. Payment, billing, refunds, and cancellations are handled by Apple using your Apple ID. You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime in your iPhone's Settings, under your name, then Subscriptions.

If you're under 18 and your Apple ID is part of Family Sharing, your parent or guardian may be asked to approve the purchase on their device. That approval happens inside Apple's systems, not ours.

Teens and parents

We built FinForge for teens, so we try to collect as little as possible and explain it plainly. Parents and guardians of a FinForge user can contact us to ask what information we hold about their teen, or to ask us to delete it. We'll verify the request before acting on it, to protect the account.

Deleting your account

In the app: go to Settings, tap Delete Account at the bottom, and confirm. Your account and everything in it are deleted right away: your profile, budgets, spending, goals, and learning progress.

If you can't get into the app, email us from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Premium subscription, because Apple manages billing. Cancel it first in your iPhone's Settings, under your name, then Subscriptions. Apple keeps its own records of App Store purchases.

We keep your information only while you have an account. After you delete it, copies may remain in our hosting provider's encrypted backups for a short time, typically up to 7 days, before they're overwritten.

Keeping it safe

Information is encrypted while it travels between the app and our servers. Our database is set up so each account can only read and change its own information, and the parts that control Premium status can only be changed by our server after it checks with Apple. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will tell affected users if a breach puts it at risk.

Your rights

You can see and change most of your information directly in the app, and you can delete all of it at any time. You can also contact us to:

  • get a copy of the information we have about you,
  • correct something that's wrong, or
  • delete your account and its information.

Some states, including California, give residents additional privacy rights. We don't sell or share personal information for advertising, and we won't treat you differently for using your rights. To make a request, contact us.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If a change affects how we use your information in a significant way, we'll let you know in the app before it takes effect.

Contact us

Questions about your privacy, a request about your information, or need help with FinForge? Email us at support@tryfinforge.com.
